About Tanmoy

Tanmoy Chakraborty holds the Rajiv Khemani Young Faculty Chair in AI and is a Professor in the Department of Electrical Engineering and the Yardi School of Artificial Intelligence at the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi (IIT Delhi), India. Before that, he served as an Assistant Professor (May 2017 - Dec 2021) and an Associate Professor (Jan 2022 - Sep 2022) in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at IIIT Delhi, India and an Associate Professor (Sep 2022 - June 2026) in the Department of Electrical Engineering at IIT Delhi. He also served as the head of the Infosys Centre for Artificial Intelligence at IIIT Delhi. He has received numerous national and international fellowships -- Ramanujan (2018-2023), DAAD (2018), PECFAR Fellowship (2023), ELISE visiting professor (2024), and Humboldt (2025-2026), INSA Young Associate (2025). Tanmoy obtained his Ph.D. as a Google PhD scholar from the Department of CSE, Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur, India, in September 2015. Following this, he joined the University of Maryland, College Park, as a Postdoctoral Researcher. His broad research interests include Natural Language Processing and Social Computing. His current research focuses on designing efficient, adaptable, secure, and interpretable language models, spanning LLM efficiency, interpretability, privacy, and security, with applications in mental healthcare and cyber-informatics.

He is the founder and PI of the Laboratory for Computational Social Systems (LCS2) research group. He has authored two textbooks -- "Social Network Analysis" and "Introduction to Large Language Models", on which he has been offering NPTEL courses. He is an ACM Distinguished Speaker (2023-2025) and a senior member of ACM and IEEE. He is involved in the organizing/program committee of almost all top AI/ML/NLP conferences, including his role as the Program Chair of ASONAM'24 and EMNLP'25 and local chair of AACL'25. He is also serving as an Action Editor of TACL, CL and TALLIP, and an Editor-in-Chief of ACL Rolling Review (ARR).

Tanmoy's research is supported by both industries like Google, Facebook, Adobe, Microsoft, LinkedIn, Samsung, Flipkart, Logically, JP Morgan, and IBM, and government agencies like DST/ANRF, SERB, MHA, ICMR and DRDO. Apart from this, he also received numerous faculty awards from industries including Google, Adobe, IBM, Accenture and JP Morgan. He was one of the project directors whose leadership secured 1000 Million INR for IIIT Delhi to establish iHub Anubhuti-IIITD Foundation, a Technology Innovation Hub. He is the PI of two mega multi-instituional project grants, funded by ICMR and ANRF on Responsible Medical AI and AI4Science, respectively.

Research Interests

Tanmoy broadly works in Natural Language Processing, Social Computing, and Graph Neural Networks. Currently, his research focuses on building economical, interpretable and faithful language models and applying them specifically to mental health counselling and cyber-informatics. Some of his research areas are highlighted below.

Large Language Models

    His research on Large Language Models (LLMs) is motivated by the objective of enriching the capabilities of frugal language models and making these ‘black-box’ models more transparent and understandable. His lab is currently working on enhancing their reasoning capabilities and in-context learning abilities, grounding them in task-specific knowledge, and optimizing their efficiency for real-world deployment through model compression techniques like knowledge distillation. Another focal point of his research is to understand how LLMs function. His lab has been looking into the internal mechanisms of these models that manifest emergent abilities like Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning, multilingual in-context learning, etc.

Cyber-informatics

    His social computing research focuses on various aspects of malicious online content, particularly hateful and misinformed posts. His work is not limited to text and topological features but extends to multimodal setups like harmful memes. Through analysis of dangerous content on the web, he aims to model and counter the spread of such content, empowering content moderators with semi-automated tools.

AI for Mental Health

    He focuses on various aspects of mental health, broadly on counselling-based therapy, intervention, and psychotherapy using AI and NLP. He has contributed to the identification of dialogue acts and summarization of mental health counselling conversation. This work is further extended to aspect-based summarization based on counselling components. Other works include the identification of mental health symptoms aided by gender information from Reddit conversation data, which he extended further to mental health dialogue generation guided by dialogue acts. Apart from these works, his lab is constantly expanding its horizon by shifting towards cognitive and neuropsychiatry by collaborating with mental health experts. Furthermore, he is also venturing into the menstrual health, sexual health and suicide intervention-related domains by collaborating with NGOs for the grass-root level reach and outsourcing.
